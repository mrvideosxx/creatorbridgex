User Data Deletion

Last updated: May 8, 2026

How to Request Data Deletion

If you wish to delete your data from CreatorBridge, you can do so by following these steps:

  • Disconnect your social media accounts from within the CreatorBridge app
  • Send an email to support@creatorbridge.app with the subject line "Data Deletion Request"
  • Include your account email address or username in the request

What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon receiving your request, we will delete all data associated with your account, including:

  • Authentication tokens and credentials
  • Account connection information
  • Publishing history and logs
  • Any cached profile information

Timeline

Data deletion requests will be processed within 30 days of receipt. You will receive a confirmation email once your data has been fully removed.

Contact

For questions about data deletion, contact us at: support@creatorbridge.app
