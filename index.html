FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about CreatorBridgeX.

Is CreatorBridgeX free?

CreatorBridgeX offers a free tier for individual creators managing up to 3 accounts. Premium plans are available for teams and agencies managing more accounts.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Authentication tokens are stored locally on your device. We never store your passwords or transmit credentials to external servers. You can revoke access at any time.

Which platforms are supported?

Currently TikTok and Facebook Pages. Instagram support is coming soon. We use official platform APIs for all integrations.

Do I need to disclose AI-generated content?

Yes, platforms like TikTok require creators to disclose AI-generated content. CreatorBridgeX includes a built-in toggle that automatically sets the is_ai_generated flag when publishing.

Can I schedule posts for later?

Absolutely. You can set a specific date and time for each post, and CreatorBridgeX will publish it automatically when the time comes.
