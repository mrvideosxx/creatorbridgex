Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 8, 2026

1. Introduction

CreatorBridgeX ("we", "our", or "the App") is a content management tool that helps creators publish their video content. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our application.

2. Information We Collect

When you connect your social media accounts to CreatorBridgeX, we may access:

  • Your account display name and profile information
  • List of pages or profiles you manage
  • Authentication tokens required to publish content on your behalf

We do not collect, store, or process any personal data beyond what is strictly necessary to perform the publishing functions you request.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information solely to:

  • Authenticate your identity with third-party platforms (Facebook, TikTok)
  • Display your pages/profiles so you can select where to publish
  • Publish video content that you explicitly choose to post
  • Schedule content at times you specify

4. Data Storage and Security

All authentication tokens are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit your credentials to any external server. Your data remains under your control at all times.

5. Third-Party Services

CreatorBridgeX interacts with the following third-party APIs:

  • Meta (Facebook) — Graph API for publishing to Facebook Pages
  • TikTok — Content Posting API for publishing videos

Your use of these platforms is also governed by their respective privacy policies.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties. We do not use your data for advertising or analytics purposes.

7. Data Retention

Authentication tokens are stored only as long as you maintain your connection to the platform. You may revoke access at any time by disconnecting your account within the app or revoking permissions directly on the respective platform.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we store about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Revoke platform permissions at any time
  • Disconnect your accounts from CreatorBridgeX

9. Children's Privacy

CreatorBridgeX is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@creatorbridge.app
